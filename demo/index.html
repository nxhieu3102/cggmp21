Demo setup. All parties run in this browser tab, one Web Worker each; the page only routes their messages. Every party keeps its secrets (key share, Paillier key) inside its own worker and nothing is persisted. A real deployment runs parties on separate devices and needs authenticated and encrypted channels plus a reliable broadcast between them.
